🧩 Mission 1 — Crack the Physiology Code

Question: Arrange the bone-adaptation mechanism correctly.

Mechanical loading→Osteocyte mechanosensing→Bone-cell signalling→Bone adaptation
Example answer: Exercise places mechanical strain on bone. Osteocytes detect this strain and coordinate signals to bone-forming and bone-resorbing cells. With appropriate loading, nutrition and recovery, bone tissue adapts over time.

🔗 Mission 2 — Match the Mechanism

Question: Match the terms to their physiological functions.

TermCorrect function
Stroke volumeBlood ejected per heartbeat
Growth plateLongitudinal bone growth
Oxidative phosphorylationAerobic ATP production
Motor coordinationMovement skill refinement
Example explanation: Stroke volume contributes to cardiac output (heart rate × stroke volume). Differences in stroke volume between children and adults can affect their cardiovascular response to exercise.

🔬 Mission 3 — Explain the WHY

Question: Why can children become stronger without substantial muscle hypertrophy?

Example answer: Strength gains can arise from improved motor-unit recruitment, neural coordination and movement technique. These adaptations increase force production without requiring large changes in muscle size. Supervised resistance training can therefore develop strength and movement competence in children.

🩺 Mission 4 — Solve the Clinical Case

Case: A 10-year-old girl becomes tired during repeated shuttle runs but enjoys moderate cycling.

Physiology: Shuttle runs require acceleration, deceleration and changes in direction, potentially creating greater relative metabolic and neuromuscular demands than comfortable cycling.

Investigate: Ask about running intensity, symptoms, rest periods, previous training and environment.

Exercise consideration: Begin with tolerable aerobic exercise, then gradually add short shuttle-running drills with adequate recovery and technique coaching.

Reasoning: Exercise should be tailored to maturation, skill, fitness and individual symptoms rather than copying adult performance expectations.
⭐ Answer format: Physiological feature → Mechanism → Exercise implication. For clinical cases, include what you would assess before making recommendations.
